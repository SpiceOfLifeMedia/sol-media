READ THIS FIRST — important:
Your current Outlook signature has old social icons (Facebook, Instagram etc.) left over from a previous version. They will stay there unless you remove them.

Step 1: In Outlook signature editor, click in the signature box, press Cmd+A / Ctrl+A, press Delete. Box must be empty.
Step 2: Then come back here and copy/paste the new signature below.
How to install (60 seconds):
1. Make sure Outlook signature box is empty (see above).
2. Click anywhere inside the white box below.
3. Press Cmd+A (Mac) or Ctrl+A (Windows) to select all.
4. Press Cmd+C / Ctrl+C to copy.
5. Click into the empty Outlook signature box → paste with Cmd+V / Ctrl+V.
6. Tick "Set default for new messages" and "Set default for replies and forwards".
7. Hit Save.
